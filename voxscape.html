Free tools, optional upgrades

The Essentials collection, selected voice effects, voice-over recording and 720p/1080p export are available without Pro. Locked sounds can be previewed before purchase.

Voxscape Pro includes all voice effects, all sound collections, external audio import and 4K export. Weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions are offered. Any eligible trial and its renewal price are shown before purchase.

Individual paid collections are also available as one-time purchases. Nature Complete unlocks Wild Earth, Blue Horizons and Storm Chaser. Buying a collection does not unlock Pro-only features.

Your media stays with you

Video effects, audio mixing and exports are processed on your device. See the Voxscape Privacy Policy for media storage, permissions and purchase details.
