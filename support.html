ClueQuest

Coins did not arrive after a purchase? Coins are added automatically as soon as Apple confirms the purchase, including purchases approved later through Ask to Buy or interrupted by the app closing. Reopen ClueQuest while connected to the internet. If the coins still do not appear, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from your Apple receipt. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.
