Lumen Text Riddle support

Need a hand with a riddle or an app issue?

support.manar@gmail.com

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, what happened and any error message. For a riddle issue, include its text or level identifier. Do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information.

App issues

Describe the steps that led to the problem and what you expected to happen. If possible, attach a screenshot with personal information removed.

Purchase questions

If a purchase did not appear as expected, contact support with the product name and approximate purchase time. Apple handles billing and refund requests through Report a Problem. Never send your Apple Account password or full payment details.

Before deleting the app

Contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app to troubleshoot a problem. Deletion can remove locally stored progress.
