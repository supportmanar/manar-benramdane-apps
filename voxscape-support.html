Getting started

Create a project from Photos or Files, select a voice effect, then use Add sound to choose a bundled sound or record a voice-over. External audio import requires Pro.

My original audio is silent

Select the Voice row and check Mute original audio and Voice volume. Also check the volumes and mute settings of added sound clips.

Purchased collection is still locked

Use Restore on the paywall while signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase. An individual collection purchase unlocks that collection, not premium voice effects, external audio import or 4K export. Nature Complete unlocks the three nature collections.

Manage subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel Pro in your Apple Account's Subscriptions settings. Refund requests are handled by Apple through Report a Problem.

Export problems

Check available device storage and permission to save to Photos. Try exporting at 720p or 1080p. 4K and projects using Pro-only features require an active Pro entitlement. If export fails, contact support with the error message, export quality and video duration.

Projects and deletion

Projects are stored locally. Export videos you want to keep before deleting a project or the app. Voxscape does not provide its own account-based project synchronization.
