Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This shared policy covers apps and games published by Manar Benramdane, including Lumen Text Riddle, Voxscape, Water Bottle Sorting and historical use of the discontinued ClueQuest. App-specific sections describe different practices; advertising disclosures for games do not automatically apply to Voxscape.

Support and general practices

We do not currently provide user profiles or operate our own Firebase Analytics implementation. If you contact support, we receive your email address and information you choose to send, such as device details and an issue description, to investigate and respond. Do not send sensitive media or payment details. You may contact us about deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Advertising and purchases

Games may use Google AdMob and offer in-app purchases. Ad providers may process device identifiers, IP addresses, diagnostics, performance information and advertising interactions under their policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Apple or the applicable store processes purchases; we do not receive full payment-card details.

Unity

Some games may use Unity. Using the engine does not necessarily mean Unity services are enabled. Disclosures will be updated if additional services are introduced.

Lumen Text Riddle

Lumen does not require an account and does not use analytics. Coins, solved riddles, unlocked collections, daily-gift streaks, sound and haptic settings, and IDs of delivered purchases are stored on your device; we do not receive them. Deleting the app removes local progress, which the app does not recover or transfer to another device.

Rewarded videos are optional and are served by Google AdMob, for example for coins, clues, letters or doubled rewards. Where required, including the EEA, UK and Switzerland, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. Change your choice under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Lumen does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access IDFA.

Coin packs are consumable purchases processed by Apple. Verified transaction information is used to grant coins once. Challenge a friend creates a riddle image locally and opens the system share sheet; sharing occurs only when you choose a destination.

Voxscape

Voxscape processes imported videos, recordings, effects and exports on your device. Project files and rendered media are stored locally. We do not operate a media-upload service for Voxscape or receive project media through the editing workflow.

Microphone access is used for optional voice-over recording. System pickers let you select video and audio. Photo permissions are used where needed to access videos and save exports. Manage permissions in device Settings.

Apple processes subscriptions and sound-pack purchases. StoreKit transaction and entitlement information is used to unlock features and restore eligible purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.

Saving to Photos and sharing are initiated by you. Destinations process shared content under their own policies. Device backups, photo synchronization and iCloud Drive imports depend on your device and Apple settings; local processing does not exclude system backups.

You can delete projects in the app. Removing the app can remove local media. Manage exported copies, shared files and backups separately. Voxscape does not provide account-based project synchronization or media recovery.

ClueQuest — discontinued

These disclosures remain for previous users. ClueQuest does not require an account or use analytics. Coins, completed levels, stars, unlocked clues and categories, reward counters, sound and haptic settings, and delivered purchase IDs are stored locally. Reset Progress or deleting the app removes this data; the app cannot recover or transfer it.

ClueQuest uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded videos. Where required, it uses Google's consent platform before ads are requested; choices are available under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Ads may preload after permission to request them, so Google may process device and usage information even without a completed video. ClueQuest does not request tracking permission or access IDFA. Consumable coin purchases use Apple's verified transaction information to grant coins once.

Children and changes

Our apps are not knowingly directed at children where prohibited by law. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns. Before adding profiles, analytics or other services, we will update this policy and relevant store disclosures.
