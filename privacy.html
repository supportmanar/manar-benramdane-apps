Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy applies to mobile games published by Manar Benramdane, including ClueQuest, Water Bottle Sorting, Lumen Text Riddle, and future games, unless a game provides a separate policy.

Information we currently handle

We do not currently provide user profiles or operate our own Firebase Analytics implementation. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, game name, device details, and description of the issue, so we can respond.

Advertising and purchases

Games may use Google AdMob to display advertising and may offer in-app purchases through the relevant app store. AdMob and app-store providers may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising information, diagnostics, performance information, and interactions as described in their own documentation and privacy policies. Purchases are processed by the applicable app-store provider; we do not receive your full payment-card details.

Unity

Some games may be developed with Unity. Using the Unity engine does not necessarily mean that Unity services are enabled. If a game uses Unity services or other third-party services, the applicable disclosures will be updated.

Lumen Text Riddle

Lumen Text Riddle does not require an account and does not use analytics. Your coins, solved riddles, unlocked collections, daily-gift streak, settings (sound and haptics), and the IDs of delivered coin purchases are stored only on your device. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it, and it cannot be recovered or moved to another device.

Rewarded videos. Videos are optional and only play when you choose to watch one, for example to earn free coins, get a clue or letter, or double a reward. They are served by Google AdMob. In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Lumen asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Lumen does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so it does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Coin purchases. Coin packs are one-time, consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. The app uses Apple's verified transaction information only to add coins to your wallet once per purchase.

Sharing. When you tap “Challenge a friend”, Lumen creates a riddle image on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Future changes

We may add user profiles, Firebase Analytics, or other services in the future. Before doing so, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures.

Children

Our games are not knowingly directed at children where prohibited by applicable law. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns.

Contact

Questions or requests: support.manar@gmail.com.
