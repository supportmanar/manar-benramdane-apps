Voxscape Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy applies specifically to Voxscape, the video voice and audio editor published by Manar Benramdane. The advertising disclosures for our other games do not automatically apply to Voxscape.

Videos, audio and projects

Voxscape processes imported videos, voice recordings, sound effects and exports on your device. Project files, imported media and rendered audio are stored locally in the app's storage. We do not operate a media-upload service for Voxscape or receive your project media through the editing workflow.

Permissions

Microphone access is used for optional voice-over recording. Selected videos and audio can be imported through system pickers. Photo library permissions are used where needed to access videos and save finished exports. You can manage permissions in device Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and sound-pack purchases are processed by Apple. Voxscape uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock purchased features and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive full payment-card information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to information Apple processes.

Sharing and device services

Saving to Photos or sharing a video is initiated by you. The destination you choose may process the exported content under its own policies. Device backups, iCloud Drive imports and photo synchronization are controlled by your Apple and device settings; local processing does not mean that your device never backs up files.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive media or payment details. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Deletion

You can delete projects in the app. Removing the app can remove its local projects and media. Exported copies, shared files and device or cloud backups must be managed separately. Voxscape does not provide an account-based media recovery service.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if Voxscape's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact support.manar@gmail.com.
